# Slotted Sunglasses Quest

## Overview
Two-step quest to craft Slotted Sunglasses by paying a broker and then a maker.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Sunglasses Trader** — Alberta (`alberta.gat`) **88,193**
- **Maseph** — Morroc Field 09 (`moc_fild09.gat`) **209,128**

## Requirements
**Step 1 (Trader):**
- **1 Carat Diamond** x1 (item 730)
- **Feather** x50 (item 949)
- **100,000 zeny**

**Step 2 (Maker):**
- **Sunglasses** x1 (item 2201)
- **400,000 zeny**

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Sunglasses Trader** in Alberta at **88,193**.
2. Agree to pay and hand over the **diamond**, **50 feathers**, and **100,000 zeny**.
3. Go to **Maseph** in **moc_fild09 209,128**.
4. Hand over **Sunglasses** and **400,000 zeny**.
5. Receive **Slotted Sunglasses**. The quest resets after completion.

## Reward
- **Slotted Sunglasses** x1 (item 2202)
