# Kiel Dungeon Access Quest

## Overview
Custom access to Kiel’s dungeon by paying an entry fee to Kiel Carlo.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Kiel Capry** — Prontera (`prontera.gat`) **147,177** (intro dialog)
- **Kiel Carlo** — Kiel’s School (`kh_school.gat`) **119,146** (entry NPC)

## Requirements
- **Base Level 70+**
- **10,000 zeny** entry fee per access

## Step-by-step
1. In Prontera, speak with **Kiel Capry** at **147,177** to learn the location.
2. Go to **kh_school.gat 119,146** and talk to **Kiel Carlo**.
3. Choose **“Yep.”** and meet the requirements.
4. Pay **10,000 zeny** and get warped into **kh_kiehl02**.

## Result
- Access to Kiel’s dungeon area (warp into `kh_kiehl02`).
