# Marriage Quest

## Overview
Hold a wedding ceremony in Prontera Church to marry another character.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Marry Happy** — Prontera Church (`prt_church`) **97,100**
- **Bishop Vomars** — Prontera Church (`prt_church`) **100,128**

## Requirements
- Groom: **Tuxedo** x1 and **1,300,000 zeny**
- Bride: **Wedding Dress** x1 and **1,200,000 zeny**
- Both: **Diamond Ring** x1
- Both characters must be online, in a party of two, and not already married.
- Only opposite-sex marriages are supported.

## Step-by-step
1. Go to Prontera Church and talk to **Marry Happy** at **97,100**.
2. Choose **Apply for Wedding** and complete the application for both bride and groom.
3. Form a party of two with your partner.
4. Talk to **Bishop Vomars** at **100,128**.
5. The groom speaks first and enters the bride’s exact name.
6. The bride speaks next and enters the groom’s exact name within the time limit.
7. If the names and requirements are correct, the ceremony completes and you are married.

## Reward
- Your characters become **married** and receive wedding rings.
