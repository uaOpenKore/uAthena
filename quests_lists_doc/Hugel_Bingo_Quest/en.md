# Hugel Bingo Quest

## Overview
Join the Hugel Bingo mini-game by entering the waiting room and paying the entry fee.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Bingo Waiting Room** — Hugel Bingo (`que_bingo`) **48,84**
- **Bingo Plate** — Hugel Bingo (`que_bingo`) **49,125** (plates 1–5 area)

## Requirements
- **1,000 zeny** entry fee.
- Wait for **5 players** to fill the bingo waiting room.

## Step-by-step
1. Go to the Hugel Bingo map and enter the **Bingo Waiting Room** at **48,84**.
2. When the room fills with 5 players, you are warped into the game area.
3. Step on a **Bingo Plate** (e.g., **49,125**) and pay the **1,000 zeny** fee.
4. Enter your bingo numbers and wait for the game to start.

## Reward
- Participation in the **Hugel Bingo** game (prizes depend on game results).
