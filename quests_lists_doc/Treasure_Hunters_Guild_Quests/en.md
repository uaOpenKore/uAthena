# Treasure Hunters Guild Quests

## Overview
Join the Treasure Hunters Guild in Yuno to take randomized quests and earn tokens for the guild shop.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Guild Leader (Keegan)** — Yuno Interior (`yuno_in01.gat`) **124,164**
- **Quest Manager** — Yuno Interior (`yuno_in01.gat`) **112,151**
- **Treasure Token Shop** — Yuno Interior (`yuno_in01.gat`) **150,152** (from THQS_TTShop)

## Requirements to Join
- **25,000 zeny** membership fee

## Step-by-step (Guild access)
1. Go to **yuno_in01.gat 124,164** and talk to **Keegan**.
2. Choose **“I want to be a Treasure Hunter”** and pay **25,000 zeny**.
3. Membership flag is set; you can now take quests and use the shop.

## Step-by-step (Quests)
1. Talk to the **Quest Manager** at **112,151**.
2. Accept a randomized quest (the system prevents taking multiple quests at once and enforces a cooldown).
3. Follow the quest NPC’s dialog and instructions in the target map.
4. Return to the **Quest Manager** to complete and receive **Treasure Tokens**.

## Notes
- The quest pool contains many NPCs across Midgard (delivery, hunting, and investigation tasks).
- Rewards are **Treasure Hunter Tokens** used in the guild shop.

## Result
- Access to repeatable Treasure Hunter quests and token shop.
