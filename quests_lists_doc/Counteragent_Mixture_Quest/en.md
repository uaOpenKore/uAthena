# Counteragent & Mixture Quest

## Overview
Unlock Morgenstein’s services and craft Counteragent or Mixture by delivering rare solutions and paying a fee.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Merchant Louitz** — Alberta Interior (`alberta_in`) **130,54**
- **Aure Dupon** — Geffen (`geffen`) **181,114**
- **Chemist Morgenstein** — Geffen Interior (`geffen_in`) **141,140**

## Unlock Steps
1. Talk to **Merchant Louitz** in **alberta_in 130,54**.
2. Choose **“More about solutions”** to learn about the mad scientist.
3. Go to **Aure Dupon** in **geffen 181,114**.
4. Choose **“More about Morgenstein”** to get his location.
5. Visit **Morgenstein** in **geffen_in 141,140** and choose **“Ask about research”**.
6. After his explanation, the crafting option is unlocked.

## Crafting Requirements
### Counteragent
- **Alcohol** x1 (item 970)
- **Detrimindexta** x1 (item 971)
- **Empty Bottle** x1 (item 713)
- **3,000 zeny**

### Mixture
- **Alcohol** x1 (item 970)
- **Karvodailnirol** x1 (item 972)
- **Empty Bottle** x1 (item 713)
- **4,000 zeny**

## Crafting Steps
1. Talk to **Morgenstein** and choose **“Make a new one”**.
2. Select **“Counteragent”** or **“Mixture”**.
3. Choose **“Make”** and pay the fee with required items.
4. Receive the crafted solution.

## Rewards
- **Counteragent** x1 (item 973)
- **Mixture** x1 (item 974)
