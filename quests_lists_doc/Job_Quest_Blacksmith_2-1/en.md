# Blacksmith Job Quest (2-1)

## Overview
Advance from Merchant to Blacksmith by completing the Blacksmith Guild’s exams and errands across multiple cities.

## NPCs and Coordinates

## Requirements
- Merchant job requirements (job level/skills as required).

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Guildsman** at **ein_in01 18,28** to apply for the Blacksmith exam.
2. You will be assigned a **branch of tasks** (errands/production/exam questions).
3. Visit the assigned NPCs in various towns (Geffen/Morroc/Lighthalzen/Payon/Hugel) and complete their requests.
4. Return to the Blacksmith Guild in Einbroch after each task to advance your exam stage.
5. After finishing the required stages, return to the main Guildsman to complete the promotion.

## Result
- Job change to **Blacksmith**.
