# Sphinx Mask Quest

## Overview
Buy a Sphinx Mask from the Turban Thief in Morroc for a negotiated price.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Turban Thief** — Morroc (`morocc.gat`) **140,156**

## Requirements
- **500,000 – 1,000,000 zeny** (price depends on negotiation)

## Step-by-step
1. Speak to **Turban Thief** in Morroc at **140,156**.
2. Choose a payment option (1,000,000 → 750,000 → 500,000).
3. Pay the selected amount.
4. Receive the **Sphinx Mask**.

## Reward
- **Sphinx Mask** x1 (item 7114)
