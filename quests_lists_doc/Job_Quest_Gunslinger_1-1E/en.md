# Gunslinger Job Quest (1-1E)

## Overview
Become a Gunslinger by completing Master Miller’s tasks and proving yourself to the Wise Bull Horn in Payon.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Master Miller** — Gunslinger HQ (`que_ng`) **152,167** (main NPC)
- **The Wise Bull Horn** — Payon (`payon.gat`) **184,65**

## Requirements
- Novice Job Level **10+** (JobLevel > 9).

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Master Miller** at **que_ng 152,167** and apply.
2. Accept his request to **deliver a letter** to **The Wise Bull Horn** in Payon.
3. Go to **payon 184,65**, speak to **The Wise Bull Horn**, and follow his errands.
4. Complete the assigned tasks and return to **Master Miller**.
5. After approval, receive your job change.

## Result
- Job change to **Gunslinger**.
