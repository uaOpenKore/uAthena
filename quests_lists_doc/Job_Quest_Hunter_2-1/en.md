# Hunter Job Quest (2-1)

## Overview
Advance from Archer to Hunter by completing tests in the Hunter Guild.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Hunter Guildsman** — Hunter Guild (`hu_in01`) **386,373** (main NPC)
- **Guild Receptionist** — `hu_in01` **382,382**
- **Hunter** — Payon Interior (`payon_in03`) **131,7** (option test)
- **Hunter** — Payon Interior (`payon_in02`) **21,31** (option test)
- **Guide / Manager** — Test map (`job_hunte`) **178,32** / **1,1**

## Requirements
- Archer job requirements (job level/skills per guild).

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Hunter Guildsman** at **hu_in01 386,373** and apply.
2. Follow the receptionist’s instructions and enter the test waiting room.
3. Complete the Hunter test on `job_hunte` (trap/maze/skill trial).
4. If you receive the Payon test task, speak to the Hunter NPCs in Payon and complete their requests.
5. Return to the guild and finish the promotion.

## Result
- Job change to **Hunter**.
