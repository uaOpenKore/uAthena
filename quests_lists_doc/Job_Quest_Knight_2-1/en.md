# Knight Job Quest (2-1)

## Overview
Advance from Swordman to Knight by completing the Chivalry tests in Prontera’s Chivalry Headquarters.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Chivalry Captain** — Prontera Interior (`prt_in`) **88,101** (main NPC)
- **Sir Andrew** — Prontera Interior (`prt_in`) **75,107**
- **Sir Siracuse** — Prontera Interior (`prt_in`) **71,91**
- **Sir Windsor** — Prontera Interior (`prt_in`) **79,94** (test supervisor)
- **Lady Amy** — Prontera Interior (`prt_in`) **69,107**
- **Sir Edmond** — Prontera Interior (`prt_in`) **70,99**
- **Sir Gray** — Prontera Interior (`prt_in`) **87,92**
- **Knight Windsor** — Test map (`job_knt`) **89,106**

## Requirements
- Base/Job requirements for Knight (Swordman with required job level/skills).

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Chivalry Captain** at **prt_in 88,101** and apply for the Knight exam.
2. You will be directed to different **Chivalry NPCs** (Andrew, Siracuse, Lady Amy, Sir Edmond, Sir Gray). Follow each NPC’s instructions and complete their requests.
3. When told, report to **Sir Windsor** at **prt_in 79,94** to enter the **knight test arena**.
4. Inside `job_knt`, follow **Knight Windsor**’s instructions and clear the test (combat/trial sequence).
5. Return to the Captain to finalize the promotion.

## Result
- Job change to **Knight**.
