# Alchemist Job Quest (2-2)

## Overview
Advance from Merchant to Alchemist by completing experiments and exams in Aldebaran Alchemist Guild.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Alchemist Guildsman** — Alchemist Guild (`alde_alche`) **27,185** (main NPC)
- **Fastidious Alchemist** — `alde_alche` **175,107**
- **Studying Man** — `alde_alche` **13,15**
- **Experiment Expert** — `alde_alche` **79,19**
- **Master Alchemist** — `alde_alche` **101,184**
- **Chief Researcher** — `alde_alche` **145,19**

## Requirements
- Merchant job requirements (job level/skills as required).

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Alchemist Guildsman** at **alde_alche 27,185** and apply.
2. Complete the assigned tasks: research questions, delivery, and experiment steps.
3. Follow instructions from the **Fastidious Alchemist**, **Experiment Expert**, and **Chief Researcher**.
4. Return to the **Master Alchemist** for final approval.
5. Finish at the Guildsman to complete promotion.

## Result
- Job change to **Alchemist**.
