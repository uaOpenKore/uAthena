# Rebirth (Transcendent) Quest — Valkyrie Realm

## Overview
Rebirth from a 2nd class into High Novice in Valhalla. This is required before taking High 1st/Transcendent class changes.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Valkyrie** — Valhalla (`valkyrie`) **48,86**

## Requirements
- Base Level **99** and Job Level **50** on a 2nd class character.
- Must have **0 zeny**, **0 items**, and no cart/falcon/peco.
- **0 unspent skill points**.

## Step-by-step
1. Go to **valkyrie 48,86** and talk to **Valkyrie**.
2. If you still have zeny, items, cart, falcon, or peco, she sends you back to empty them.
3. If you have unspent skill points, spend them and return.
4. Confirm the rebirth dialogue. The Valkyrie resets you to **High Novice**.
5. You receive starter items and are warped to the appropriate city.

## Result
- Job change to **High Novice** (rebirth completed).
