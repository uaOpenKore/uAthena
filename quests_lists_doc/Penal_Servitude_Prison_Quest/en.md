# Penal Servitude (Prison) Quest

## Overview
Prisoners can pay for release or work it off by collecting mushrooms in the prison farm.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Chief Warder (Saddeus)** — Prison (`sec_pri.gat`) **36,58**
- **Overseer (Oliver)** — Prison Farm (`sec_in02.gat`) **137,57**

## Options to Get Released
### 1) Pay for release
- Fee scales with number of imprisonments (minimum **1,000,000 zeny**)
- Pay cash or via Kafra bank account

### 2) Work off release
- You are warped to the prison farm and must collect **Orange Net Mushroom**.
- The required amount is calculated based on your prison count and ASPD.

## Step-by-step (Work off)
1. Talk to **Chief Warder** at **36,58** and choose **“Let me to work off my freedom!”**
2. You are warped to **sec_in02** and stripped of equipment.
3. Hunt **Toadstool** mobs to collect **Orange Net Mushroom**.
4. Return to **Overseer Oliver** at **137,57**.
5. Hand over the required number of mushrooms to be released to Izlude.

## Result
- Release to **Izlude 105,112** after payment or mushroom hand-in.
