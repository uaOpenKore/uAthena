# Crusader Job Quest (2-2)

## Overview
Advance to Crusader by completing the trials of the Crusader Order.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Senior Crusader** — Prontera Castle (`prt_castle`) **45,169** (main NPC)
- **Man in Anguish** — `prt_castle` **164,32**
- **Crusader** — Prontera Church (`prt_church`) **95,127**
- **Patron Knight** — `prt_castle` **35,151**
- **Waiting Room** — Test map (`job_cru`) **24,187**

## Requirements
- Swordman job requirements (job level/skills as required).

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Senior Crusader** at **prt_castle 45,169** and apply.
2. Follow the assigned tasks (church visit, castle tasks, trials).
3. Enter the test map `job_cru` through the waiting room.
4. Complete the combat trials in the test map.
5. Return to the Senior Crusader to complete promotion.

## Result
- Job change to **Crusader**.
