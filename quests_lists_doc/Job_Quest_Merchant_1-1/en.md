# Merchant Job Quest (1-1)

## Overview
Become a Merchant by passing a multi-part test involving accounting and deliveries.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Merchant** — Alberta Interior (`alberta_in`) **53,43** (main NPC)
- **Merchant Guildsman** — Alberta Interior (`alberta_in`) **28,29**
- **Student** — Morroc Interior (`morocc_in`) **140,102**
- **Guild Staff** — Geffen Interior (`geffen_in`) **155,122**
- **Kafra Employee** — Prontera (`prontera`) **248,42**

## Requirements
- Novice Job Level **9+** and **Basic Skills** learned.

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Merchant** at **alberta_in 53,43** and apply for Merchant.
2. You are assigned a **random test path** (accounting or delivery). Follow the NPC’s exact instructions.
3. If assigned **accounting**, you must enter the **correct calculation code** when prompted.
4. If assigned **delivery**, take the provided test item and deliver it to the target NPC (Geffen/Morroc/Prontera), then return with confirmation.
5. After completing all stages, return to the Merchant NPC to receive the job change.

## Result
- Job change to **Merchant**.
