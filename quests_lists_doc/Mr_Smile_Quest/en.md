# Mr. Smile Quest

## Overview
Craft a Mr. Smile mask by turning in simple materials to a Smile Assistant in any major town.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Smile Assistant** — Alberta (`alberta`) **113,53**
- **Smile Assistant** — Aldebaran (`aldebaran`) **136,135**
- **Smile Assistant** — Geffen (`geffen`) **119,107**
- **Smile Assistant** — Izlude (`izlude`) **129,118**
- **Smile Assistant** — Morroc (`morocc`) **158,97**
- **Smile Assistant** — Payon (`payon`) **168,225**
- **Smile Assistant** — Prontera (`prontera`) **157,187**

## Requirements
- **Jellopy** x10 (item 909)
- **Fluff** x10 (item 914)
- **Clover** x10 (item 705)

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to any **Smile Assistant** listed above.
2. Choose **“Construct a Mr. Smile”**.
3. Hand over **10 Jellopy**, **10 Fluff**, and **10 Clover**.
4. Receive the **Mr. Smile** mask.

## Reward
- **Mr. Smile** x1 (item 2278)
