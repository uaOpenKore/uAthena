# Acolyte Job Quest (1-1)

## Overview
Complete a penance mission by visiting one of three ascetics and return to Prontera Church.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Father Mareusis** — Prontera Church (`prt_church`) **184,41** (main NPC)
- **Father Rubalkabara** — Prontera Field 03 (`prt_fild03`) **365,255**
- **Mother Mathilda** — Morroc Field 07 (`moc_fild07`) **41,355**
- **Father Yosuke** — Prontera Field 00 (`prt_fild00`) **208,218**

## Requirements
- Novice Job Level **9+** and **Basic Skills** learned.

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Father Mareusis** at **prt_church 184,41** and apply.
2. You will be assigned **one** ascetic to visit (random):
   - **Father Rubalkabara** (NE of Prontera) at **prt_fild03 365,255**
   - **Mother Mathilda** (SW of Prontera, near Morroc) at **moc_fild07 41,355**
   - **Father Yosuke** (NW of Prontera) at **prt_fild00 208,218**
3. Find the assigned ascetic, talk to them, and receive confirmation of your penance.
4. Return to **Father Mareusis** and complete the job change.

## Result
- Job change to **Acolyte**.
