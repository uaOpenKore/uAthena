# Six New Hats Quest

## Overview
Four brothers in Prontera Interior craft six new hats if you bring the correct materials.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Zac** — Prontera Interior (`prt_in.gat`) **130,66** (Bongun Hat)
- **Blac** — Prontera Interior (`prt_in.gat`) **162,131** (Crescent Hairpin)
- **Jac** — Prontera Interior (`prt_in.gat`) **53,56** (Kafra Band, Panda Hat)
- **Pac** — Prontera Interior (`prt_in.gat`) **45,113** (Poring Hat, Sphinx Hat)

## Hat Recipes
**Bongun Hat (Zac):** 10 Amulets (609), 1 Cobaltblue Dyestuff (978), 1 Munak Hat (2264)

**Crescent Hairpin (Blac):** 1 Heart Hairpin (5041), 10 Steel (999)

**Kafra Band (Jac):** 1 Silk Ribbon (10007), 1 Wild Flower (10009)

**Panda Hat (Jac):** 10 Steel (999), 200 Bear Footskin (948)

**Poring Hat (Pac):** 1 Poring Doll (741), 300 Jellopy (909)

**Sphinx Hat (Pac):** 25 Steel (999), 1 Darkgreen Dyestuff (979), 1 Lemon Dyestuff (976), 150 Fabric (1059), 2 Gold (969)

## Step-by-step
1. Go to **prt_in.gat** and choose the brother who makes the hat you want.
2. Use **“Information”** if you need the recipe.
3. Gather the listed items and select **“Join”** → desired hat.
4. Hand in the materials to receive the hat immediately.

## Rewards
- **Bongun Hat** (5046)
- **Crescent Hairpin** (5048)
- **Kafra Band** (5020)
- **Panda Hat** (5030)
- **Poring Hat** (5035)
- **Sphinx Hat** (5053)
