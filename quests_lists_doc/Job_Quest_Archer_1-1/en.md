# Archer Job Quest (1-1)

## Overview
Become an Archer by submitting the application and bringing enough trunks to the Archer Guild.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Archer Guildsman** — Payon Interior (`payon_in02`) **64,71**

## Requirements
- Novice Job Level **9+** and **Basic Skills** learned.
- Trunks for grading (any mix):
  - **Tree of Archer 1** (item 1066) = 5 points each
  - **Tree of Archer 2** (item 1067) = 3 points each
  - **Tree of Archer 3** (item 1068) = 2 points each
  - **Wooden Block** (item 1019) = 1 point each
- Total score must be **25+** points.

## Step-by-step
1. Go to **payon_in02 64,71** and talk to **Archer Guildsman**.
2. Choose **“I want to be an Archer”** and **apply**.
3. Gather trunks to reach **25+ points** (see scoring above).
4. Return and choose to have your items appraised.
5. If your total score is 25+ points, the trunks are taken.
6. You are promoted to **Archer** and receive a bow reward.

## Result / Reward
- Job change to **Archer**.
- Bow reward and arrows based on trunk types.
