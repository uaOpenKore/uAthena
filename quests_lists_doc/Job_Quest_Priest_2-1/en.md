# Priest Job Quest (2-1)

## Overview
Advance from Acolyte to Priest by completing trials at Prontera Church and the priest test map.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **High Bishop** — Prontera Church (`prt_church`) **16,41** (main NPC)
- **Sister Cecilia** — `prt_church` **27,24**
- **Peter S. Alberto** — Test map (`job_prist`) **24,187**

## Requirements
- Acolyte job requirements (job level/skills as required).

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **High Bishop** at **prt_church 16,41** and apply.
2. Follow instructions from **Sister Cecilia** and proceed to the test map.
3. In `job_prist`, complete the trials (zombie/mummy rooms and boss trials) as instructed by **Peter S. Alberto**.
4. After clearing the trials, return to the High Bishop to complete promotion.

## Result
- Job change to **Priest**.
