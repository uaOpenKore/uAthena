# Star Gladiator Job Quest (2-1E)

## Overview
Advance from Taekwon to Star Gladiator by gathering rare elemental ores, then meditating with the Sun, Moon, and Stars instructors.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **MooHyun** — Payon (`payon.gat`) **215,102** (starts quest)
- **Warper Man** — Comodo (`comodo.gat`) **172,231** (warps to test area)
- **Gladiator of the Sun** — Test map (`job_star.gat`) **29,33**
- **Gladiator of the Moon** — `job_star.gat` **95,34**
- **Gladiator of the Stars** — `job_star.gat` **161,34**

## Requirements
- Class **Taekwon** with Job Level **40+**.
- For warp: **Flame Heart** x1 (994), **Mystic Frozen** x1 (995), **Rough Wind** x1 (996), **Great Nature** x1 (997).
- Final test: **Star Dust** x1 (1000), **Star Crumb** x1 (1001), and **0 unspent skill points**.

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **MooHyun** at **payon 215,102** and accept the Star Gladiator path.
2. Go to **Comodo 172,231** and talk to the **Warper Man**.
3. Hand over the four elemental ores to open the warp to `job_star`.
4. Speak to **Gladiator of the Sun** → **Gladiator of the Moon** → **Gladiator of the Stars** in order.
5. At the final instructor, hand over **Star Dust** and **Star Crumb** and ensure you have **0 skill points**.
6. You are promoted to **Star Gladiator**.

## Result
- Job change to **Star Gladiator**.
