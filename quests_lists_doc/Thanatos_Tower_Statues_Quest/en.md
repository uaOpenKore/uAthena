# Thanatos Tower Statues Quest

## Overview
Puzzle-style quest where you speak to statues/spirits in order to unlock the next Thanatos Tower floors.

## NPCs and Coordinates (key checkpoints)
- **Statue** — `tha_t08.gat` **69,106** (intro text)
- **Thalos** — `tha_t09.gat` **17,158**
- **Mediane** — `tha_t09.gat` **84,99** (warps to `tha_t10`)
- **Melkor** — `tha_t10.gat` **130,159**
- **Zebrus** — `tha_t10.gat` **160,98**
- **Veriaelle** — `tha_t10.gat` **99,97** (warps to `tha_t11`)
- **Throtre** — `tha_t11.gat` **16,85**
- **Klermaz** — `tha_t11.gat` **85,16**
- **Mihane** — `tha_t11.gat` **16,16**
- **Seranes** — `tha_t11.gat` **85,85** (warps to `tha_t12`)

## Step-by-step
1. Read the **Statue** at `tha_t08 69,106` to understand the challenge.
2. On `tha_t09`, talk to **Thalos** first, then **Mediane** to get warped to `tha_t10`.
3. On `tha_t10`, speak **Melkor** → **Zebrus** → **Veriaelle** in order to unlock the warp to `tha_t11`.
4. On `tha_t11`, speak **Throtre** → **Klermaz** → **Mihane** → **Seranes** to unlock the warp to `tha_t12`.
5. Continue following the spirit order on the next floors (the script enforces order and resets if you talk out of sequence).

## Reward / Result
- Access to higher Thanatos Tower floors via statue warps.
