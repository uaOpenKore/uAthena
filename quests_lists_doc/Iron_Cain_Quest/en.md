# Iron Cain Quest

## Overview
Trade Orcish Vouchers and a Heroic Emblem for the Iron Cain headgear in Payon.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Iron Cain Quest** — Payon (`payon.gat`) **109,118**

## Requirements
- **Orcish Voucher** x200 (item 931)
- **Heroic Emblem** x1 (item 968)

## Step-by-step
1. Go to **Payon 109,118** and talk to **Iron Cain Quest** NPC.
2. Confirm you want the item.
3. Hand over **200 Orcish Vouchers** and **1 Heroic Emblem**.
4. Receive **Iron Cain**.

## Reward
- **Iron Cain** x1 (item 2266)
