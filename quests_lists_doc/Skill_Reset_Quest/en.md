# Skill Reset Quest

## Overview
Reset your skill points for eligible first-class or expanded characters at the Hypnotist in Prontera.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Hypnotist** — Prontera (`prontera`) **146,232**

## Requirements
- **Base Level 40 or lower**.
- Job must be **Swordman, Acolyte, Mage, Thief, Archer, Merchant, Taekwon, Gunslinger, or Ninja**.
- Must **not** be a baby class or first-class transcendent.
- **Weight must be 0** and no **pushcart** equipped.
- Service is **free**.

## Step-by-step
1. Go to Prontera and talk to **Hypnotist** at **146,232**.
2. Select **Skill Reset** and confirm.
3. If your level and job qualify, and your weight is 0 with no pushcart, the reset is performed.

## Reward
- **Skill points reset** for the character.
