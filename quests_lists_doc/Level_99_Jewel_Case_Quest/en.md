# Level 99 Jewel Case Quest

## Overview
Special quest for Base Level 99 2nd class characters to earn a Jewel Case after rebirth.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **La Fenn** — Lighthalzen (`lighthalzen.gat`) **108,229**

## Requirements
- Base Level **99** and **2nd class** (non-transcendent)
- At least **1,000,000 Base EXP** at level 99 to activate the riddle
- Rebirth (become younger) after activation

## Step-by-step
1. At **Base 99 (non-trans)**, talk to **La Fenn** in Lighthalzen (**108,229**).
2. If you have less than **1,000,000 Base EXP**, she will ask you to gain more EXP (suggests Seals or Poporing).
3. Once you have enough EXP, talk again to activate her riddle (quest flag).
4. **Rebirth** (get younger) to reset level.
5. Return to **La Fenn** after rebirth to claim the reward.

## Reward
- **Jewel Case** x1 (item 12106)
