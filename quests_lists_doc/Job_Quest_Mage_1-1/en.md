# Mage Job Quest (1-1)

## Overview
Become a Mage by creating a randomly assigned Mixed Solution in the Geffen Mage Guild.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Mage Guildsman** — Geffen Interior (`geffen_in`) **164,124** (application/test)
- **Mixing Machine** — Geffen Interior (`geffen_in`) **164,112**
- **Bookshelf (Guide Book)** — Geffen Interior (`geffen_in`) **177,112**

## Requirements
- Novice Job Level **9+** and **Basic Skills** learned.
- Create the assigned **Mixed Solution No. 1–4** (randomly assigned).

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Mage Guildsman** at **geffen_in 164,124**.
2. Choose **“I want to be a Mage”** and **sign up**.
3. The NPC assigns **Mixed Solution No. 1–4** and gives you an **Empty Cylinder**.
4. Read the **Guide Book** at **geffen_in 177,112** to see the required ingredients for your solution.
5. Use the **Mixing Machine** at **geffen_in 164,112** and follow the menus to select solvent and ingredients.
6. When you successfully create the solution, return to the **Mage Guildsman**.
7. If the solution matches your assigned number, you are promoted to **Mage**.

## Result
- Job change to **Mage**.
