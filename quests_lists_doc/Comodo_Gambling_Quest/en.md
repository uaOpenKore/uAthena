# Comodo Gambling Quest

## Overview
Trade a Diamond for a random equipment prize at Kachua in Comodo.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Kachua** — Comodo (`comodo`) **219,160**

## Requirements
- **Diamond** x1 (item 732)
- Inventory weight must be **below 50%**.

## Step-by-step
1. Go to Comodo and talk to **Kachua** at **219,160**.
2. Offer a **Diamond** when asked.
3. Choose a prize category (Weapon, Armor, Garment, Helmet, Shoes, Shield).
4. If you meet the weight requirement, you receive a random item from that category.

## Reward
- **Random equipment item** from the chosen category.
