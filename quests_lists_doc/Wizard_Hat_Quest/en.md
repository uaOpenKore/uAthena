# Wizard Hat Quest

## Overview
Craft a Wizard Hat by trading rare materials and zeny to the hat craftsman in Geffen.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Wizard Hat Dude** — Geffen (`geffen.gat`) **126,107**

## Requirements
- **Magician Hat** x1 (item 5045)
- **Bathory Card** x1 (item 4119)
- **200,000 zeny**

## Step-by-step
1. Go to Geffen and talk to **Wizard Hat Dude** at **126,107**.
2. Choose **“Sure am!”** to confirm crafting.
3. The NPC checks your inventory and zeny. If you have all items, they are removed.
4. Receive your **Wizard Hat**.

## Reward
- **Wizard Hat** x1 (item 2252)
