# Assassin Job Quest (2-1)

## Overview
Advance from Thief to Assassin by passing the Assassin Guild’s trial maze and interviews.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Guildsman** — Assassin Guild (`in_moc_16`) **19,33** (main NPC)
- **Guildsman** — `in_moc_16` **25,90** (test entry)
- **Test Guide / Standby Room** — `in_moc_16` **21,165**
- **Guildmaster** — `in_moc_16` **149,80** (final approval)

## Requirements
- Thief job requirements (job level/skills as required).

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Guildsman** at **in_moc_16 19,33** and apply.
2. Enter the standby room at **in_moc_16 21,165** when the test is available.
3. Complete the Assassin test maze (traps and timing) as instructed by the **Test Guide**.
4. After clearing the trial, proceed to the **Guildmaster** for final approval.
5. Return to the initial NPC to finalize the promotion.

## Result
- Job change to **Assassin**.
