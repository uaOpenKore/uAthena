# Swordman Job Quest (1-1)

## Overview
Become a Swordman by applying at the Swordman Association and completing the test hall trial.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Swordman** — Izlude Interior (`izlude_in`) **74,172** (application/interview)
- **Swordman** — Izlude Interior (`izlude_in`) **62,170** (pre-test guidance)
- **Swordman** — Izlude Interior (`izlude_in`) **30,175** (test entry dialogue)
- **Test Hall Staff** — Izlude Interior (`izlude_in`) **30,163** (warps to test hall)
- **Test Hall Staff / Medic** — Test map (`job_sword1`) **230,242**, **230,204**, **223,167** (inside test rooms)

## Requirements
- Novice Job Level **9+** and **Basic Skills** learned.

## Step-by-step
1. Go to **izlude_in 74,172** and talk to **Swordman**.
2. Choose **“Job Change”** and **sign up**.
3. If you meet the Basic Skill/Job Lv requirement, proceed to the test.
4. Talk to the **Test Hall Staff** at **izlude_in 30,163** to enter `job_sword1`.
5. Inside the test hall, follow staff instructions and clear the **three rooms** (avoid traps/finish objectives).
6. Use Medics if needed (they heal in test rooms).
7. After clearing the rooms, talk to the final staff NPC to complete the test and receive your job change.

## Result / Reward
- Job change to **Swordman**.
