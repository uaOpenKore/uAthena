# Dead Branch Quest

## Overview
Trade wood materials for a Dead Branch, with a small chance for a Bloody Branch.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Mister Mobry** — Niflheim (`niflheim.gat`) **204,179**

## Requirements
- **Log** x3 (item 7201)
- **Wooden Heart** x1 (item 7189)
- **Trunk** x5 (item 1019)
- **Wooden Gnarl** x1 (item 7222)

## Step-by-step
1. Speak to **Mister Mobry** in Niflheim at **204,179**.
2. Select **“Make me a Dead Branch!”**.
3. Hand over the required wood items.
4. Receive a **Dead Branch** (rarely a **Bloody Branch**).

## Reward
- **Dead Branch** x1 (item 604), with a 0.1% chance for **Bloody Branch** (item 12103)
