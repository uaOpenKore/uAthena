# Soul Linker Job Quest (2-2E)

## Overview
Advance from Taekwon to Soul Linker by helping the Kid in Morroc and speaking to the Lost Souls.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Kid** — Morroc Interior (`morocc_in.gat`) **174,30** (starts quest)
- **Lost Soul #1** — Test map (`job_soul.gat`) **30,35**
- **Lost Soul #2** — `job_soul.gat` **25,30**
- **Lost Soul #3** — `job_soul.gat` **30,25**
- **Kid** — `job_soul.gat` **35,30** (final job change)

## Requirements
- Class **Taekwon** with Job Level **40+**.
- Item requirements to help the Kid:
  - **3-Carat Diamond** x1 (item 748)
  - **Witherless Rose** x1 (item 732)
  - **Immortal Heart** x1 (item 929)
- **0 unspent skill points** before warping to `job_soul`.

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Kid** at **morocc_in 174,30** and agree to help.
2. Collect the three items and return to the Kid.
3. Ensure you have **0 skill points**, then accept the warp to `job_soul`.
4. Speak to the Lost Souls in order:
   - **Lost Soul #1** (30,35)
   - **Lost Soul #2** (25,30)
   - **Lost Soul #3** (30,25)
5. Talk to the **Kid** at **35,30** to receive the job change.

## Result
- Job change to **Soul Linker**.
