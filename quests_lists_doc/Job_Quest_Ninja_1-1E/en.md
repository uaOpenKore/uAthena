# Ninja Job Quest (1-1E)

## Overview
Become a Ninja by accepting Akagi’s invitation in Alberta and completing Cougar Gai’s training in Amatsu.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Akagi** — Alberta (`alberta`) **30,65** (sends you to Amatsu)
- **Cougar Gai** — Ninja Village (`que_ng`) **30,65** (main trainer)
- **Suspicious Guy** — Einbroch (`einbroch`) **184,194** (later step)

## Requirements
- Novice Job Level **10+** (JobLevel > 9).

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Akagi** at **alberta 30,65** and choose to walk the path.
2. Akagi warps you to **Amatsu**; enter the Ninja village (`que_ng`).
3. Speak to **Cougar Gai** at **que_ng 30,65** and follow the training dialog.
4. Complete the assigned errands (includes visiting **Suspicious Guy** in **einbroch 184,194**).
5. Return to Cougar Gai and finish the trial to receive the job change.

## Result
- Job change to **Ninja**.
