# Lord Knight Job Quest (2-1a)

## Overview
Advance to **Lord Knight** in the Valkyrie Realm after rebirth by meeting the High 1st class requirement and speaking to the class NPC.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Lord Knight** — Valkyrie Realm (`valkyrie`) **44,39**

## Requirements
- **High 1st class** (Job_Swordman_High).
- **Job Level 40+**.
- **0 unspent skill points**.

## Step-by-step
1. After rebirth, become your **High 1st class** and reach **Job Level 40**.
2. Travel to the **Valkyrie Realm** and find the **Lord Knight** NPC at **44,39**.
3. Confirm the promotion. If you have unspent skill points, use them first.
4. Receive your job change to **Lord Knight**.

## Result
- Job change to **Lord Knight**.
