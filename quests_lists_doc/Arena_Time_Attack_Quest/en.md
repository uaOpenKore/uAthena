# Arena Time Attack

## Overview
Enter the Prontera Arena and attempt time-attack monster challenges.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Arena Entrance** — Prontera (`prontera`) **160,185**
- **Time Attack** — Arena Waiting Room (`prt_are_in`) **29,79**

## Requirements
- None.

## Step-by-step
1. Go to Prontera and speak with **Arena Entrance** at **160,185**.
2. Choose **Let’s go!** to warp to the arena waiting room.
3. Talk to **Time Attack** at **29,79**.
4. Pick a challenge level to enter the arena.
5. Defeat all monsters before the time limit to succeed.

## Reward
- Access to the **Arena Time Attack** challenge.
