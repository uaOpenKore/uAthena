# Elven Ears Quest

## Overview
Craft Elven Ears by bringing specific headgear and materials in Geffen.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Elven Ears Quest** — Geffen (`geffen.gat`) **127,49**

## Requirements
- **Kitty Band** x1 (item 2213)
- **Elder Pixie Moustache** x20 (item 1040)
- **Animal Skin** x20 (item 919)

## Step-by-step
1. Go to **Geffen 127,49** and talk to **Elven Ears Quest** NPC.
2. Choose **“Requirements”** to confirm the item list.
3. Gather all required items.
4. Select **“Make Item”** to hand them over.
5. Receive **Elven Ears**.

## Reward
- **Elven Ears** x1 (item 2286)
