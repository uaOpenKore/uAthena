# Monk Job Quest (2-2)

## Overview
Advance from Acolyte to Monk by completing physical and combat trials at the Monk Temple.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Guarding Monk** — Monk Temple (`prt_monk`) **59,247** (entry)
- **Sensei Moohae** — Monk Interior (`monk_in`) **99,58** (main NPC)
- **Touha** — `prt_monk` **251,255**
- **Boohae** — `prt_monk` **57,179**
- **Door Keeper** — `prt_monk` **199,169**
- **Bashu** — Test map (`monk_test`) **329,61**
- **Apprentice Monk** — `monk_test` **386,388**

## Requirements
- Acolyte job requirements (job level/skills as required).

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Guarding Monk** at **prt_monk 59,247** to enter.
2. Speak to **Sensei Moohae** at **monk_in 99,58** and apply.
3. Complete assigned tasks from Touha/Boohae and open the test via Door Keeper.
4. Enter `monk_test` and finish the combat/training stages under **Bashu**.
5. Return to the Sensei to complete promotion.

## Result
- Job change to **Monk**.
