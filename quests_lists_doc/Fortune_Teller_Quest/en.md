# Fortune Teller Quest

## Overview
Receive a tarot-style monster card reading in Payon by offering a voluntary zeny fee.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Fortune Teller (Lhimetorra)** — Payon Interior (`payon_in03`) **117,128**

## Requirements
- Any amount of **zeny** offered as a fee (you choose the amount).

## Step-by-step
1. Go to Payon Interior and talk to **Fortune Teller** at **117,128**.
2. Choose **I would like a tarot card reading.**
3. Enter the amount of zeny you want to offer.
4. The fortune teller performs a random monster card reading based on your selection.

## Reward
- A **fortune reading** (no items are granted).
