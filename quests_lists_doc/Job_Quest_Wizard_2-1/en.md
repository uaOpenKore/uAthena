# Wizard Job Quest (2-1)

## Overview
Advance from Mage to Wizard by completing elemental tests in the Wizard Guild.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Wizard Guildsman** — Geffen Tower (`gef_tower`) **111,37** (main NPC)
- **Gloomy Wizard** — Geffen Tower (`gef_tower`) **102,24** (exam info)
- **Arena Assistant / Waiting Room** — Test map (`job_wiz`) **50,165**
- **White Dog** — Geffen Tower (`gef_tower`) **107,36** (test hints)

## Requirements
- Mage job requirements (job level/skills as required by the guild).

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Wizard Guildsman** at **gef_tower 111,37** to apply.
2. Receive instructions and proceed to the test area via the waiting room on `job_wiz`.
3. Complete the elemental rooms in order:
   - **Room of Water**
   - **Room of Earth**
   - **Room of Fire**
4. Each room requires correct spell usage/interaction; failing sends you to a fail room.
5. After clearing all rooms, return to the guild NPC to complete the promotion.

## Result
- Job change to **Wizard**.
