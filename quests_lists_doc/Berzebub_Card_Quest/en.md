# Berzebub Card Quest

## Overview
One-time quest to craft a Berzebub Card by delivering rare materials.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Old Woman** — Prontera (`prontera.gat`) **164,177**

## Requirements
- **Emperium** x25 (item 714)
- **Witch Starsand** x100 (item 1061)
- **Needle of Alarm** x200 (item 1095)
- **Worn Out Scroll** x10 (item 618)
- **Bible** x2 (item 1551)
- **Wand of the Occult** x1 (item 1614)
- **Opal** x20 (item 727)

## Step-by-step
1. Speak to **Old Woman** in Prontera at **164,177**.
2. Choose to hear the requirements and confirm you want the card.
3. Hand over all listed items in one go.
4. Receive **Berzebub Card** (quest can only be completed once per character).

## Reward
- **Berzebub Card** x1 (item 4145)
