# Thief Job Quest (1-1)

## Overview
Become a Thief by passing a mushroom collection test via the Thief Guild in Morroc.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Thief Guide** — Thief Guild (`moc_prydb1`) **39,129** (main NPC)
- **Comrade** — Thief Guild (`moc_prydb1`) **42,133** (mushroom judge)
- **Mr. Irrelevant** — Morroc Ruins (`moc_ruins`) **141,125** (warp to mushroom farm)

## Requirements
- Novice Job Level **9+** and **Basic Skills** learned.

## Step-by-step
1. Go to **moc_prydb1 39,129** and talk to **Thief Guide**.
2. Apply for the job change and accept the mushroom test.
3. The test requires collecting **Orange Net Mushroom** and **Orange Gooey Mushroom**.
4. Talk to **Mr. Irrelevant** at **moc_ruins 141,125** to be warped to the farm.
5. Hunt and collect mushrooms until you reach **25 points** (Net = 3 pts, Gooey = 1 pt).
6. Return to the guild and talk to **Comrade** at **moc_prydb1 42,133** to have mushrooms graded.
7. If you pass, return to **Thief Guide** to complete the job change.

## Result / Reward
- Job change to **Thief**.
