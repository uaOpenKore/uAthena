# Taekwon Job Quest (1-1E)

## Overview
Become a Taekwon by training under Phoenix in Payon and completing a short spiritual trial.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Phoenix** — Payon (`payon.gat`) **157,141**

## Requirements
- Novice Job Level **9+** and **Basic Skills** learned.
- Gain **+1 Base Level** after accepting training.
- Have **0 unspent skill points** when returning after the level-up.

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Phoenix** at **payon 157,141** and accept training.
2. Gain **one Base Level** and return.
3. If you still have skill points, you must spend them before continuing.
4. Answer Phoenix’s **series of questions** (spiritual trial).
5. After passing the Q&A, Phoenix changes your job.

## Result
- Job change to **Taekwon**.
