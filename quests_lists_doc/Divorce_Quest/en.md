# Divorce Quest

## Overview
End a marriage by paying a fee to Deviruchi in Niflheim.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Deviruchi** — Niflheim Interior (`nif_in`) **190,112**

## Requirements
- Your spouse must be **online**.
- **2,500,000 zeny**

## Step-by-step
1. Go to Niflheim Interior and talk to **Deviruchi** at **190,112**.
2. Confirm that you want a divorce.
3. If your spouse is online and you have the required zeny, the fee is taken.
4. The divorce is processed and your marriage status is removed.

## Reward
- Your character becomes **single** (marriage is dissolved).
