# BtoBB (Bloody Branch) Quest

## Overview
Custom exchange to convert 100 Dead Branches into a Bloody Branch.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Thernopol Drunk Boy** — Aldebaran (`aldebaran.gat`) **112,97**

## Requirements
- **Dead Branch** x100 (item 604)
- **Sumptuous Feast** x1 (item 12082)
- **Black Cat Doll** x1 (item 7206)
- **Alcohol** x1 (item 970)
- **100 zeny**

## Step-by-step
1. Go to **Aldebaran 112,97** and talk to **Thernopol Drunk Boy**.
2. Agree to help and provide the items and zeny.
3. The NPC removes the materials and gives you a **Bloody Branch**.

## Reward
- **Bloody Branch** x1 (item 12103)
