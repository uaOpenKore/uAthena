# Lord Kaho Horns Quest

## Overview
High-end custom quest to craft Kaho Horns by collecting many boss drops and zeny.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Lord Kaho's Servant** — Geffen (`geffen.gat`) **115,107**

## Requirements
Boss drops and dolls:
- Skull (Dark Lord) x1 (754)
- Heroic Emblem (Orc Hero) x1 (968)
- Evil Horn (Baphomet) x1 (742)
- Red Frame (Doppelganger) x1 (750)
- Smoking Pipe (Eddga) x1 (751)
- Fang of Garm x1 (743)
- Mother’s Nightmare (Maya) x1 (752)
- Sphinx Hat (Osiris) x1 (5053)
- Diamond Ring (Mistress) x1 (2613)
- Poring Doll x1 (741)
- Chonchon Doll x1 (7020)
- Baphomet Doll x1 (7036)
- Osiris Doll x1 (2268)
- Rocker Doll x1 (734)
- Yoyo Doll x1 (923)
- Racoon Doll x1 (7005)
- Spore Doll x1 (753)
- **Emperium** x3 (714)
- **5,000,000 zeny**

## Step-by-step
1. Go to **Geffen 115,107** and talk to **Lord Kaho's Servant**.
2. Confirm you want the Kaho Horns.
3. Bring **all listed items and 5,000,000 zeny** at once.
4. The NPC removes the materials and gives you the **Kaho Horns**.

## Reward
- **Kaho Horns** x1 (item 5013)
