# Fashion Glasses Quest

## Overview
Daily-style quest to craft Fashionable Glasses in Izlude.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Fashion Glasses Quest** — Izlude (`izlude.gat`) **135,96**

## Requirements
- **Jack'a Dandy** x1 (item 2271)
- **Scarlet Dyestuff** x1 (item 975)

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to **Fashion Glasses Quest** NPC at **135,96** in Izlude.
2. Choose **“Requirements”** to see the item list.
3. Collect **Jack'a Dandy** and **Scarlet Dyestuff**.
4. Select **“Make Item”** and hand over the items.
5. Receive **Fashionable Glasses**.

## Reward
- **Fashionable Glasses** x1 (item 5047)
