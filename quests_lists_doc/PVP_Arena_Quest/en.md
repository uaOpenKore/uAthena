# PVP Arena Access

## Overview
Enter the PvP fight squares through the Gate Keeper and PVP Narrators in major towns.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **PVPNarrator** — Morroc Inn (`morocc_in`) **144,138**
- **Gate Keeper** — Morroc Inn (`morocc_in`) **144,142**
- **PVP Narrator** — Alberta Inn (`alberta_in`) **22,146**
- **PVPNarrator** — Prontera Inn (`prt_in`) **56,140**
- **PVPNarrator** — Geffen Inn (`geffen_in`) **67,63**
- **PVPNarrator** — Payon Inn (`payon_in01`) **142,50**

## Requirements
- **Yoyo Mode** entry: **500 zeny** and **Base Level 31+**.
- **Event Mode** entry: **Admission Ticket** (item 7028) or **Viewing Ticket** (item 7029).

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to any **PVPNarrator** to review PvP modes and rules.
2. Go to the **Gate Keeper** in Morroc Inn at **144,142**.
3. Choose a mode:
   - **Yoyo Mode**: pay 500 zeny and meet the level requirement to enter.
   - **Event Mode**: present an admission or viewing ticket to enter.
4. You are warped into the appropriate PvP room.

## Reward
- Access to the **PvP fight square**.
