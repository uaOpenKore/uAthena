# Rogue Job Quest (2-2)

## Overview
Advance from Thief to Rogue by completing the Rogue Guild’s tests and tasks.

## NPCs and Coordinates
- **Thug** — Morroc (`morocc`) **100,104** (entry NPC)
- **Rogue Guildsman** — Rogue Guild (`in_rogue`) **363,122** (main NPC)
- **Mr. Smith** — `in_rogue` **376,23** (test branch)
- **Hermanthorn Jr.** — `in_rogue` **272,135** (test branch)

## Requirements
- Thief job requirements (job level/skills as required).

## Step-by-step
1. Talk to the **Thug** at **morocc 100,104** to access the Rogue Guild.
2. In `in_rogue`, talk to **Rogue Guildsman** at **363,122** and apply.
3. Complete the written/oral test and task branches assigned (skills/quiz/errands).
4. Follow instructions from NPCs such as **Mr. Smith** or **Hermanthorn Jr.**
5. Return to the **Rogue Guildsman** to finalize the job change.

## Result
- Job change to **Rogue**.
